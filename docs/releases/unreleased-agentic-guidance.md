# Agentic work guidance

## Changed

- The built-in `decision-input` instruction directs every Session to resolve blockers and issues itself: diagnose the root cause, try every local compliant fix, recover from failed checks, conflicts and environment errors, and keep working toward the goal without stopping at milestones, status reports or questions that evidence can settle. Permission, guardrail and human-only approval requests and missing secrets or credentials still go through their native controls.
