# YCoding v0.10.20

## Fixed

- Waking during automatic-answer evaluation returns questions, forms, and subagent questions to ordinary human input instead of creating a sleeping review. Stale automatic answers remain invalid, and sleeping Sessions retain their review-box behavior.
- Question settlement preserves human replies and sleep-entry conversions completed during the refreshed sleep lookup, avoiding duplicate reviews and pending-question notices for those settled requests.
- A form waiting for human input after wake is cancelled when its caller is interrupted.
- Purpose-specific YOLO helper model pins take precedence over the ordinary decision helper. Unsupported or unavailable routes and recoverable response failures use a bounded chain of explicitly configured helpers, preserving exact variants and profiles. Typed decisions support the ChatGPT Codex HTTP adapter with existing credentials.
- An unconfigured decision helper inherits the current Session model, including subsequent model switches. Explicit helper and role pins keep their documented precedence; an unsupported inherited route requires review rather than automatic approval.
- Decision preflight, model resolution, attempts, joined waits, and cache rechecks share one deadline. Computing cache identity does not refresh unused credentials, and results are cached only while their model and account identity remain unchanged.
- Permission and guardrail results under YOLO are discarded after authority changes, including revocation followed by restoration. Permission settlement rechecks current denies and ceilings. Genuine hard reviews still require human approval; model confidence does not grant authority.
- Disabling baseline standard-match verification preserves deterministic matches even with YOLO enabled. Independent YOLO grading retains its existing approval limits. Withheld verdicts and policy-required handoffs are not described as model-chosen escalations.
- The TUI keeps autonomy controls in the sidebar without duplicate prompt badges, status summaries, or Autonomy header summaries. Distinct waiting state, subagent counts, pending decisions, and keyboard controls remain available.
