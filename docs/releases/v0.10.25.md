# YCoding v0.10.25

## Fixed

- Settled guardrail outcomes stay in chronological conversation order instead of collecting below newer output. The TUI includes both match dismissals and decision outcomes; the web keeps its decision disclosures inside the transcript.
- After compaction, covered guardrail rows stay out of the current transcript through refresh and reconnect. Their audit records remain available, and true pending reviews retain their human decision controls.
- Muse appears in TUI and web provider quotas before its first quota sample, with an explicit next-response message instead of invented usage values. Unauthorized providers retain their status and message.
- Muse account quota observed in one workspace remains available to quota views in other workspaces on the same backend. Profiles and replacement accounts remain isolated; environment-key observations stay workspace-local.
