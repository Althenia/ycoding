# YCoding v0.10.4

## Added

- Idle Sessions are deleted automatically. A root Session and all of its subagents are removed, with their history, once every member has been idle for more than 7 days. Pinned Sessions, Sessions with an active goal, suspended or running Sessions, Sessions with unsent input, and Sessions another Session was forked from are kept. Change the number of days with `session_retention.days` in your global configuration. Freed database space is returned to disk in small batches, and attachment files are not deleted.

## Changed

- Choosing **Allow for this session** on an outside-repository guardrail review now approves that request's outside directory (and everything inside it) for the Session and its subagents, and is remembered after a restart. Hard reviews still always ask.
- Guardrails ask about work outside the repository less often: heredoc text, commit and PR message text, and URLs are no longer treated as paths, and the repository's main checkout and its other worktrees count as inside the repository.
- Long Sessions and large databases stay faster: starting each run, goal continuation, subagent completion, outstanding-failure status, and remote-compaction steps read only the messages they need, and SQLite keeps its query statistics up to date.

## Fixed

- Shell redirections written without a space, such as `cmd>/path/file`, or next to `2>&1` are reviewed when they write outside the repository.
