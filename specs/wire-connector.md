# Revision 5 connector and relay notice operations

The revision-5 connector contract covers the closed 70-operation connector surface and four relay-owned notice operations. `protocol/ycoding/v5/connector.proto` owns the distinct operations in the explicit 5000–5099 allocation range. Shared local operations retain their IDs, request/result declarations and local policies. Service declarations are descriptor metadata, not gRPC servers or an implemented dispatcher.

TypeScript exports `operationIDsByRole`; Rust exports `OPERATION_IDS_BY_ROLE`. Both are generated from the same descriptors and include empty allowlists. REMOTE_CLIENT may originate all 74 operations. RELAY may forward the 70 connector operations to the device connector. The relay executes notice operations itself. AGENT and SERVER may send responses and permitted controls, but no operation requests. LOCAL_CLIENT authority is retained on the 31 shared descriptors and is absent on connector-only descriptors. All other local operations remain LOCAL_CLIENT-only.

Request direction follows `packages/remote/src/index.ts:478–480,645–673`, `infra/cloudflare/src/relay/core.ts:554–613,754–827` and `packages/cli/src/remote-bridge.ts:305–328`. Claimed role never substitutes for authenticated ownership. The local, client and agent routes use the foundation PeerRole vocabulary. Generated allowlists do not authenticate an upgrade or authorize a Session.

## Composite workflows and notices

Provider authentication discovery and unified OAuth/command begin/status/cancel use target/attempt/profile-aware workflows without credential IDs or raw provider responses (`packages/cli/src/remote-provider-auth.ts:7–88`). Workspace inventory derives opaque advertised keys from projects, directories and Sessions (`packages/cli/src/remote-operations.ts:368–439`). Catalogs retain their composed offered selections and reference/resource URIs (`remote-operations.ts:464–493`).

Captured changes retain grouped parent/eligible-child projection and digest-bound paging (`remote-operations.ts:1265–1355`). Compaction history retains its bounded entries and totals (`:832–850`). Team economics, family shell inventory, side-chat list/create and activity retain their verified family composition, projection and admission effects (`:752–813,1370–1438`; `remote-local.ts:280–293`). Status retains root-family running, attention, outstanding and failed semantics (`remote-operations.ts:675–680,1441–1491`). Attachment upload retains ordered connection/Session-owned staging, completed references and TTL (`:102–166,705–707`). None of these workflows grants remote access to their underlying unrestricted local primitives.

Notice subscription/list pages use foundation `Notice.added` for the existing notice records and total, alongside opaque next cursor and unavailable state. Record categories, Session IDs and creation times use foundation `NoticeRecord`; no second Notice vocabulary is declared. Notice mutation logical results are no-content; their removed/cleared notifications use foundation Control Notice independently of settlement. Persistent notice totals and completion receipt watermark remain separate from Session completion acknowledgements (`packages/remote/src/index.ts:447–476,565–566,1012–1028,1097–1104`; `infra/cloudflare/src/relay/core.ts:790–826`; `foundation.proto:242–269`). Fresh notice authority, per-connection ordering, pagination and presentation remain relay obligations.

## Ownership and verification

`packages/protocol/test/connector-source.ts` freezes the allocation table. `connector-contract.ts` projects bounded remote request/result schemas and reuses existing domain declarations; `connector-materialize.ts` checks canonical bytes and refuses initialization over an existing file. Its source-owner update mode permits only domain-obligation provenance changes, not fields, tags, IDs or structural bounds.

Generate TypeScript descriptors and bindings with `bun run --cwd packages/protocol generate:v5`. Rust's protocol crate build owns its descriptors, bindings and role allowlists. Generate foundation vectors with `python3 protocol/ycoding/v5/testdata/generate.py`. The generated `manifest.tsv` stores tab-separated name, fully qualified type, `valid` or `invalid`, sender role number and exact lowercase hex bytes; empty bytes use an empty hex column. Tests decode the committed hex without invoking protoc or Python. The generator's case list is the only source for valid vectors. Repeated generation must be byte-identical. Digest-bearing negative vectors retain their individual rejection reasons.

Protocol connector coverage compares frozen allocations, per-role closure, mutation classes, field associations and metadata without importing Remote. Remote connector conformance compares complete live membership, ordered-lane mutation classes and Session requirements against those allocations. Remote and Rust connector conformance checks cover distinct input/result representations and foundation Notice reuse. Structural metadata never claims to execute the named domain obligations or implement connector dispatch, authentication, placement or live relay behavior.
