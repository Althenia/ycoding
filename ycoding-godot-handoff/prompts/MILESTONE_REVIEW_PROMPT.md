# Milestone review prompt

Review the current YCoding Office milestone against MILESTONES.md, CHECKLIST.md, docs/FIDELITY_SPEC.md, docs/TEST_PLAN.md and tracking/tasks.json.

Inspect actual code, runnable scene, tests, source provenance, logs and artifacts. Do not accept “implemented” labels as evidence. Identify which requirements passed, failed or were not run; cite the local evidence path for each. Verify that tests exercise production code, DEMO cannot mutate live state, plain prompting remains unchanged and the office never claims unsupported work or dialogue.

For M2/M4, inspect genuine Godot stills and normal-speed video. Score the fidelity rubric, check every hard criterion, and request the user's actual acceptance of those captures. Do not self-approve the visual gate or approve externally reconstructed mock footage. Inspect readability with the conversation drawer open, not only a clean office screenshot.

For M3/M5, distinguish transport fixtures, live-provider evidence, source-epoch recovery, human approval paths and native exported-app launch. Check service ownership and secret/license handling. Report concrete remaining defects and their task IDs; reopen invalidated tasks rather than lowering acceptance criteria.

Update the review/evidence and handoff records. Do not commit or publish anything unless explicitly authorized.
