Capture help

Use headphones to avoid recording remote audio twice. Microphone echo cancellation is enabled; microphone audio is never played back.

Select the Meet tab, arm a meeting with ycoding meeting or /meeting start, then press Start. Capture never starts automatically.

Stop also works with Ctrl+Shift+Y (Command+Shift+Y on Mac); configure it in Chrome extension shortcuts. Call-connection closure stops capture conservatively, including internal reconnections. Use Stop if a call ends without closing its connection.
